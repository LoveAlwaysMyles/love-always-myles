THE ART OF BELONGING · SEASON TWO

Dear you,

You do not have to arrive polished. Come as you are. Leave a little lighter.

Host · storyteller · safe-space builder

Meet your host

I’m Myles
and this is personal.

I feel deeply, love a good laugh, and usually want to know more than the surface-level answer. I love music, meaningful conversations, and the little moments that make people feel thought of.

Connection matters to me. So does learning how to care for others without forgetting myself. I’m still finding my voice, learning to trust it, and building a life that feels honest to who I am.

I created Love Always, Myles to give those conversations somewhere to live. A place where I can share what I’m learning, admit what I don’t know, and bring both my humor and my heart.

This podcast isn’t me teaching from the other side of life. It’s me living it—and inviting you into the conversation.

Love Always,
Myles

What is Love Always, Myles?

Softness with
something to say.

This is not a space where anyone has to pretend they have it all figured out.

Dear you,

I created Love Always, Myles because I know what it feels like to carry so much while quietly wishing someone would ask, “How are you, really?” And stay long enough to hear the answer.

This is my invitation to bring your whole self—the hopeful parts, the tired parts, and the parts learning to begin again.

Through honest conversations about love, grief, identity, relationships, faith, and becoming, I’m sharing what life is teaching me while I’m still living it. My hope is that my honesty makes it a little easier for you to be honest, too.

I want us to say the hard thing, celebrate the small thing, and laugh when we need it most. To grow together and remember there’s still life to be lived while we’re figuring things out.

I call this community the Lovies, and that includes you. Wherever you are in your journey, I hope you find connection, a little more kindness for yourself, and a reason to stay.

There’s room for you here, Lovie.
